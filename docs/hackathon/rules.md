# International Track rules, plain text

Transcribed from `international-topics-rules-en.docx` on 10 October 2026. The docx governs where the two differ. Contact: fintechathon.g-ican.com, info@ican-x.com.

## 1. Theme

The theme of the International Track is "AI as a Financial Participant": each team designs and builds an AI agent capable of completing real tasks in a financial scenario, on one of six topics.

## 2. Topics

1. Topic A, Personal Finance Assistant: budgeting, bill analysis and spending insights for individual users.
2. Topic B, Wealth Advisory Agent: product comparison, client risk profiling and advisory dialogue, producing compliant and explainable asset allocation suggestions.
3. Topic C, Cross border and Student Finance Assistant: real financial pain points of international students and cross border users, such as remittances, tuition payments, credit building and multi currency management. **Lifeline is entered under Topic C.**
4. Topic D, Insurance Assistant: policy questions, coverage gap analysis and claims guidance.
5. Topic E, SME Finance Copilot: financing option matching, cash flow management and alerts, and business financial analysis.
6. Topic F, Agent Wallet: an AI wallet agent that executes on chain operations, with a challenge bonus of 0 to 5 points.

## 3. Scoring, the same for every topic

| Criterion | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Task completion | 40% |
| Security and compliance | 30% |
| Innovation and interaction | 30% |
| Challenge bonus, on chain exploration | 0 to 5 points |

## 4. Eligibility and teaming

1. Open to full time students, undergraduate, master's or doctoral, enrolled at universities or research institutions outside China, including outside Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan.
2. Teams of 2 to 5 students. Each person may join only one team across the Domestic and International Tracks.
3. English is the official language. Submissions, presentations and reviews are in English.
4. At least 60% of a team's members should be non Chinese nationals. The Organising Committee assigns the track.

## 5. Submission

Registration and submission share one deadline: **20 October 2026, 23:59 Beijing time (UTC+8)**. The rules give the equivalents 16:00 UTC, 17:00 London, 09:00 US Pacific, 01:00 Tokyo on 21 October and 03:00 Sydney on 21 October. A bilingual reminder email is sent 24 hours before.

The checklist:

1. Technical documentation: system architecture, core algorithm description, security design.
2. Presentation deck, for a final presentation of no more than 10 minutes.
3. Demo video, optional, no more than 5 minutes.
4. Source code, with README, GitHub repository link, deployment instructions and test cases.
5. Security self assessment report: permission tier implementation and known risk list.
6. Execution evidence. For a topic that is not on chain: simulated account or sandbox operation logs, and the demo video.

A submission may be updated any time before the deadline; the latest version counts. Nothing is accepted after it.

## 6. Review and final

1. Late October: online review by WeBank industry experts and university scholars.
2. By 31 October: finalists announced.
3. 20 November: check in and WeBank headquarters visit, Shenzhen.
4. 21 to 23 November: Grand Final and awards, Shenzhen. At least one member of each finalist team must attend in person, or the team loses award eligibility unless the absence is justified.

## 7. Fees and benefits

No registration fee. Travel and accommodation for invited finalist members are covered, for the number of members the invitation states. The International Track prize pool is RMB 250,000: first RMB 100,000, second RMB 80,000, third RMB 50,000, Best Innovation RMB 20,000, and several Excellence Awards, all before tax. Outstanding teams get fast track interviews at WeBank.

## 8. Integrity and IP

Entries must be the team's own work. Third party data, code and open source components must be properly licensed. The team keeps its IP; the hosts may use entries and team information for non commercial promotion, non exclusive and royalty free. The full terms are the Competition Rules and Privacy Policy on the official website.
