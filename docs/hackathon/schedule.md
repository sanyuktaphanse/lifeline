## Dates that no longer hold

The master plan of 14 September 2026 was written before the official rules gave a date. These dates in it are superseded:

1. "Submit by 31 Oct 2026 (deadline 2 Nov)": the official deadline is 20 October.
2. "Finalists 10 Nov": finalists are announced by 31 October.
3. "On site final at WeBank Shenzhen 21 to 22 Nov": the final runs 21 to 23 November, with check in on 20 November.
4. Week six and seven of its calendar, from 19 October to 1 November, including the fact freeze and the feature freeze on 25 October, fall after the deadline. The team has not yet agreed new dates for them; open item for the next team call.
