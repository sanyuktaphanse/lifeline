## The two wrapper sentences

English text inside a Portuguese or Chinese reply follows one of two sentences, decision 123. `quoted_in_english` comes before a knowledge entry's statement and says it is quoted in English from the source on file. `english_pending` comes before Lifeline's own English, such as a rule's reason or a rail's message, and says it is in English and its translation is pending. In English both are `{text}` and add nothing. Both reviewers asked whether the first sentence claimed a source for Lifeline's own reasons; the second sentence settles that, so the question is off the list above. Only the wording below is for a native speaker. The reviewers read `quoted_in_english` and marked its meaning correct; `english_pending` was written after the review and has not been read by either.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `english_pending` | What follows is in English and its translation is pending | `O texto a seguir está em inglês, e a tradução está pendente: {text}` | `以下内容为英文，翻译尚待完成：{text}` |
